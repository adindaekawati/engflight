Your proposal isn't losing on grammar

These three patterns show up in almost every proposal written by an Indonesian freelancer. All of them are grammatically correct. All of them lower the price.

pattern 1

Too many softeners

“Maybe I can probably finish in about two weeks or so.” You meant to be polite. What the client hears is this person isn't sure they can do it. Then the price gets pushed down.

pattern 2

Discounting before you're asked

“My rate is $80, but if that is too much I can lower it.” Nobody negotiated yet and you already moved. The client reads it as an admission that your first number was inflated.

pattern 3

“I will try”

You meant it as a polite no. The client heard yes. The unpaid work that follows isn't generosity — it's a translation error with an invoice attached.
